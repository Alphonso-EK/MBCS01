Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you apply.

What is the smallest loan I can get?

Collateral-based loans start from K1,000.

How long can I borrow for?

Choose 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks. The rates are 15%, 25%, 35% and 40%.

What can I use as collateral?

Televisions, laptops, fridges, smartphones and cars. Call or WhatsApp with details of your item.

Do you keep my collateral?

Yes. We keep it until the loan is fully repaid, then return it to you.

How is the interest worked out?

The rate for your term is applied to the amount borrowed. K2,000 for 4 weeks at 40% is K800 interest, so you repay K2,800.
